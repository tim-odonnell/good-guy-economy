    What are you researching?

    Describe the story or subject you want Good-Guy-Economy to work on — people, organizations, places, or events involved, and what you suspect or want to find out. A few sentences is plenty; you can always add more detail later.

    What does Good-Guy-Economy do?

    Once you describe a topic, Good-Guy-Economy works on it in the background on a schedule — gathering public records and news, cross-checking names and organizations against each other, and building up theories worth chasing. You control when it's actively running with the Start / Pause / Stop buttons on the next screen. Findings worth your attention show up as desktop notifications and on the dashboard.

    Good-Guy-Economy only uses public sources it can reach on its own — it can't file public-records/FOIA requests for you, but it will flag good targets for you to file yourself.
